This guide is for people who want to see how their YouTube video's title and
thumbnail compare to others.

<br />

You've spent hours filming and editing a YouTube video, but one of the biggest factors in its success happens before anyone even sees it: getting a click. A weak thumbnail and title can potentially sink a great video. I know, and [scroll to the bottom](#ps-note) if you want to see one of my videos.

<br />

You can try manually comparing your options by screenshotting the home page or recommended
and pasting your title / thumbnail on them, but that’s tedious if you need to compare
in many places (including other YouTube channels or the search page).

<br />

That’s why I built Thumbnail Swapper, a chrome extension for YouTube creators to upload
their own title and thumbnail and swap them with others directly on YouTube (shameless
plug alert).

<br />

## 1. Download Thumbnail Swapper from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/thumbnail-swapper-test-yo/pfdapjpaicgfhghbmjennmchbgabofnf?authuser=0&hl=en)

You can get started for free and without signing up.

Click this area to swap your title and thumbnail with the ones in that area.

<br />

Your thumbnail and title aren’t being judged by themselves in Photoshop or Gimp (my choice and [Pewdiepie’s new choice](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pVI_smLgTY0&t=6m5s)).
They're chosen from a lineup of other videos. And if you're not comparing them against others, you're not seeing what viewers see.
