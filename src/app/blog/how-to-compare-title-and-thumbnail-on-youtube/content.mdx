This guide is for people who want to see how their YouTube video's title and
thumbnail compare to others.

<br />

You've spent hours filming and editing a YouTube video, but one of the biggest factor in its success happens before anyone even sees it: getting a click. A weak thumbnail and title can potentially sink a great video. I know, and scroll to the bottom if you want to see one of my videos.

<br />

You can try manually comparing your options by screenshotting the home page or recommended
and pasting your title / thumbnail on them, but that’s tedious if you need to compare
in many places (including other YouTube channels or the search page).

<br />

That’s why I made Thumbnail Swapper, a chrome extension for YouTube creators to upload
their own title and thumbnail and swap them with others directly on YouTube (shameless
plug alert).

<br />

## 1. Download Thumbnail Swapper from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/thumbnail-swapper-test-yo/pfdapjpaicgfhghbmjennmchbgabofnf?authuser=0&hl=en)

<br />

You can get started for free and without signing up.

<br />

While it's still new, pro accounts are on me and send an email to [support@thumbnailswapper.com](mailto:support@thumbnailswapper.com). The free plan allows swapping on the home page, 5 uploads and
5 swaps per week. The pro plan has unlimited uploads, unlimited swaps, and swapping
on the home page, channel page, recommended, and search page.

<br />

## 5. Swap

Hover over a title and thumbnail on the YouTube page and you should see a blue background.
Click this area to swap your title and thumbnail into that area.
<br />

Your thumbnail and title aren’t being judged by themselves in Photoshop or Gimp (my choice and Pewdiepie’s new choice (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pVI_smLgTY0)). They're chosen from a lineup. If you're not previewing them against your competition, you're not seeing what your viewers see.
